I already have a job I like, so this site isn't a job hunt. It's mostly so I have one place to point people who want to know what I actually do, and a place to write about the other things I spend time on, which don't fit neatly into a CV: books, training, films, football, and whatever data side-project I'm currently obsessing over.

Expect a mix here. Some posts will be short lessons on SQL or Power BI, the kind of thing I now teach on weekends. Others will just be notes on a book I finished or a film I can't stop thinking about. If I keep it up, I'll probably end up tracking some of that as data too, which feels about right.

More soon.
